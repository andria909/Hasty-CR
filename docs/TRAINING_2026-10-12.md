# Delay-aware PPO training, 2026-10-12

Five live ladder matches exposed a latency mismatch: policy inference was only
1-4 ms, but capture and UI parsing took roughly 460-548 ms. The old simulator
executed actions immediately, so it rewarded placements aimed at a stale enemy
position. It also still offered a central Cannon pull after a building-targeting
unit had already reached the tower.

The v20 environment advances the match for 500 ms between observation and
action execution. The live safety mask predicts tracked unit positions 0.5 s
ahead. Once a building-targeter reaches row 22.5, Cannon is no longer offered
as a pull; Knight is placed on the contact instead of attempting a late
cross-lane kite.

The promoted checkpoint was fine-tuned from v19 for 120,576 PPO steps against
a 50/50 mixture of the rule-engine and meta-deck opponents. Selection used the
worst score across both opponent families.

## Paired deterministic evaluation

Both checkpoints used seed 985000 and 120 episodes per opponent under the same
500 ms delayed environment.

| Checkpoint | brain | meta | Hog share, brain | Hog share, meta |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| v19 | 37/120 (30.8%) | 9/120 (7.5%) | 13.9% | 13.8% |
| v20 | 84/120 (70.0%) | 45/120 (37.5%) | 7.4% | 11.4% |

The improvement is large on both opponent families. The remaining caveat is
that v20 attacks less often against the weaker opponent; live shadow/friendly
validation should confirm that its stronger defence does not become passive
play. Simulation results are not evidence of live win rate.
